## 2. In `body { background-color: #ecfdf5; }`, what is the selector?
the selector is the body and tells the browser which HTMl element the rule applies to.

## 3. In that same rule, what is the property and what is the value?
in that same rule,the property is background-color and the value is #ecfdf5.

## 4. Why does `.resource-card` start with a dot in CSS?

## 5. What is the difference between padding and margin in the resource card?
the difference between padding and margin in the resource card is that padding makes space inside element, between the border of the card and the content while margin makes space outside the element, between the card and the things around it.
